Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sirena Flow LLC ("Sirena Flow," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit sirenaflow.com (the "Website"), submit a form, book a demo, or otherwise communicate with us. By using the Website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Business or practice name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Website
  • Information submitted through our forms, such as the Leak Audit, Book a Demo form, and any other inquiry forms (for example, average service value, main lead source, number of locations, and preferred contact time)

We may also automatically collect limited website and technical information when you visit the Website, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, pages viewed, and referring URLs. This information is generally collected in aggregate through standard web hosting and analytics tools and is used to operate and improve the Website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Schedule and manage demos and calls
  • Provide information you have requested about our services
  • Provide customer support
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our services and Website
  • Send you SMS/text messages, but only where you have provided appropriate consent to receive them

SMS & Mobile Information

If you opt in to receive SMS/text messages from Sirena Flow, we use your mobile number solely to send you messages related to your inquiry, demo scheduling, service updates, confirmations, reminders, follow-up, and customer service.

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP. You may also contact us directly using the information in the Contact section below.

We work with third-party service providers and processors that help us operate our business, such as customer relationship management, scheduling, communication (including SMS and email delivery), and website hosting platforms. These providers may access or process information on our behalf, but only to the extent reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, and they are expected to handle your information consistent with this Policy and applicable law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, respond to your inquiries, maintain business records, and comply with our legal obligations. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Your Choices & Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us directly
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you
  • Contact us with any questions or requests regarding your information

We will respond to reasonable requests consistent with applicable law.
