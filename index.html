The Problem

Where high-ticket revenue disappears

Your ads bring in the leads. Your staff delivers the care. But in between — every unanswered call, every ignored DM, every no-show with no recovery — is revenue that walks straight to a competitor.

Missed Calls

Your line rings after hours. No answer. They call the next clinic on the list.

Unanswered DMs

A warm lead DMs you on Instagram. Six hours pass. Interest gone.

Dead-End Web Forms

Form submitted at 9pm. One auto-reply. Then nothing. No follow-up, no booking.

No-Show, No Recovery

A no-show and nobody reschedules. That slot — and that revenue — is gone.

"Still Thinking" Leads

They said they'd think about it. No follow-up happened. They booked elsewhere.

No Accountability

No tracking means no visibility — and no way to know what you're losing each month.

The Solution

Everything included in your flow

One managed service that runs your entire consultation pipeline — from first inquiry to confirmed appointment. We configure it, test it, monitor it, and improve it every month. Your team provides the personal experience; we keep opportunities moving.

◆
Capture

Prompt Response Across Your Channels

Missed calls can trigger a text-back, web forms get a prompt follow-up, and Instagram and Facebook messages are included when your accounts are connected and platform permissions allow. A chatbot on your existing website, trained on your practice-approved information, responds to visitors after hours and collects the details your team needs.

◆
Convert

Booking Assistance & Human Handoff

We manage the journey from inquiry to consultation booked, confirmed, and followed up. Your website chatbot answers routine questions and guides visitors toward a consultation — booking directly into your calendar where a supported, tested connection allows, or through your approved booking link. Medical, treatment, billing, complaint, or uncertain questions go to your team.

◆
Retain

Confirmations, Recovery, Reactivation & Reviews

Confirmations and reminders run on the appointment signals your booking system provides, and missed appointments flagged through that connection move into recovery follow-up. Past clients receive reactivation sequences timed around seasonal demand. Once a visit is confirmed complete through an agreed trigger, patients can receive a feedback or Google review request.

◆
Report

Monthly KPI Reporting

See inquiries received, response activity, bookings, recovered opportunities, and follow-up coverage — plus review requests sent and resulting review activity where that data is available. We use the numbers to keep improving your flow.

Questions

The questions you’re probably asking.

Does this replace our front desk or coordinator?

No. Sirena Flow operates as a layer on top of your existing team. Your staff stays focused on care and operations. We handle the response and follow-up that currently falls through the cracks — after hours, over DMs, or when your team is delivering services. Clinical judgment and escalated conversations stay with your staff.

Do we need to switch our EMR or booking system?

No. Sirena Flow works alongside your current booking system rather than replacing it. Where a supported, tested connection exists, we connect to it directly; otherwise we route patients through your approved booking link and hand off to your team. We confirm what’s possible for your setup during onboarding.

Is this just a website chat widget?

Website chat is included, but it’s one part of the service. Each practice gets a chatbot on its existing website, trained on your approved information, that responds after hours, answers routine questions, and guides visitors toward a consultation — with medical, billing, and uncertain questions handed to your team. Around it, Sirena Flow manages missed-call text-back, web form follow-up, Instagram and Facebook messages where connected, confirmations and no-show recovery, reactivation, review requests, and monthly reporting. We configure, test, monitor, and improve all of it.

How long does setup take?

Most practices are fully live within 7 days of onboarding. We handle the full build and testing. Your team's time investment is a 20-minute kickoff call and reviewing your flow before it goes live.

What practices are the best fit?

We're built for aesthetics and wellness brands across the US — medspas, surgical practices, cosmetic dental, and more — with active inbound volume from paid ads, social content, referrals, or organic search. If your inquiry-to-scheduled conversion rate is lower than it should be, we're likely a strong fit. The Leak Audit is a good place to start.
